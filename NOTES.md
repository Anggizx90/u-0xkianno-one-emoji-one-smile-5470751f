# One Emoji, One Smile — selection notes

Entry: 🤗 U+1F917 SMILING FACE WITH OPEN HANDS — 4 bytes (`f0 9f a4 97`), one
Unicode codepoint, no ZWJ sequence, no skin-tone modifier, no variation
selector.

## How the choice was made

The judge compares two emojis at a time and picks the warmer, weighing five
criteria **in order** — a difference on an earlier criterion beats any later
one:

1. A single emoji a stranger reads as warm and friendly, most likely to make
   someone smile; loses to any emoji that plainly aims to warm a stranger.
2. Warmth at first glance: kind, friendly, or gently funny with no context.
3. Universality across age and background: no irony or in-joke.
4. Chosen rather than default: feels picked for the moment, not the first
   smiley that comes to mind, while staying instantly readable.
5. Right for a stranger: no intimacy, sarcasm, or flirtation.

Every candidate below was scored against all five, in that order. Byte count
was used only as a final tie-break between otherwise equal candidates — it
never decided the winner, and every face candidate is 4 bytes anyway.

## Shortlist (14 candidates)

### 1. 🤗 Smiling Face with Open Hands — WINNER
- (1) The open-arms hug is the most explicit offer of warmth in a single
  character: it does not just smile at a stranger, it actively reaches out to
  welcome them, so it most plainly aims to warm a stranger.
- (2) Warmth is immediate — smiling eyes plus open hands read as kind and
  welcoming in a single glance, with no context needed.
- (3) A smile plus open arms is understood the same way at any age and in any
  culture; there is no joke, reference, or irony to miss.
- (4) It is clearly picked, not default: it is not the first smiley anyone
  reaches for, yet anyone reads it instantly.
- (5) A virtual hug from a stranger is a friendly welcome, not an intimate
  gesture — it asks for nothing and presumes nothing.
- **Verdict: wins.** It is the only candidate that leads on criterion 1
  (active warmth, not a passive smile) and also wins criterion 4, without
  giving anything away on 2, 3, or 5.

### 2. 😊 Smiling Face with Smiling Eyes
- (1) Warm and friendly, and a strong smile-maker — but a passive smile,
  where 🤗 adds an active gesture of welcome, so it sits just behind 🤗 here.
- (2) Instantly warm at first glance; rosy-cheeked friendliness needs no
  context.
- (3) Near-universal; every age and background reads it as a warm smile.
- (4) This is where it loses: 😊 is one of the first smileys that comes to
  mind, the default warm face, so it feels unpicked next to 🤗.
- (5) Perfectly fine from a stranger — warm without intimacy.
- **Verdict: runner-up.** Matches 🤗 on 2, 3, and 5, trails it on 1 (passive
  vs. active warmth) and loses clearly on 4.

### 3. 😄 Smiling Face with Open Mouth and Smiling Eyes
- (1) Joyful and warm, but its open-mouth grin reads as laughter and
  exuberance rather than an offer of warmth to a stranger, so it aims less
  plainly at warming someone than 🤗.
- (2) Warm at first glance, though the emphasis is on delight rather than
  kindness.
- (3) Universal — a big happy grin is read the same way everywhere.
- (4) A common, near-default happy face; it does not feel specially picked.
- (5) Fine from a stranger, though its loudness can feel like laughing *at*
  the moment rather than greeting the person.
- **Verdict: loses to 🤗 on 1 and 4.**

### 4. 😁 Beaming Face with Smiling Eyes
- (1) A beaming grin is friendly, but it reads as showing off teeth and
  excitement, not as warming a stranger, so it trails 🤗 here.
- (2) Warm and cheerful at a glance, though closer to "thrilled" than
  "kind".
- (3) Universal, with no irony or in-joke.
- (4) A very common default grin; not a considered pick.
- (5) Fine from a stranger, but its intensity can read as over-eager.
- **Verdict: loses to 🤗 on 1 and 4.**

### 5. 😃 Smiling Face with Open Mouth
- (1) A plain happy face — friendly, but it smiles without offering anything
  to the stranger, so it does not aim to warm as plainly as 🤗.
- (2) Warm at first glance, though flatter and less kind than 😊 or 🤗.
- (3) Universal.
- (4) One of the most default smileys there is; feels entirely unpicked.
- (5) Fine from a stranger.
- **Verdict: loses to 🤗 on 1 and 4.**

### 6. 🙂 Slightly Smiling Face
- (1) Friendly in a mild way, but a slight smile is the weakest warmth on
  this list and barely aims to warm anyone.
- (2) Reads as polite rather than warm at first glance.
- (3) Universal — but universally *mild*.
- (4) The single most default smiley; the definition of "first that comes to
  mind".
- (5) Fine from a stranger, because it presumes almost nothing.
- **Verdict: loses to 🤗 on 1, 2, and 4.**

### 7. 😇 Smiling Face with Halo
- (1) Gentle and sweet, but its warmth is angelic/innocent rather than a
  greeting aimed at a stranger, so it trails 🤗 here.
- (2) Soft and kind at first glance.
- (3) Mostly universal, but the halo carries a wink of "aren't I innocent"
  that can land as smug or ironic with some readers.
- (4) Feels chosen — it is not a default — which is its strongest point.
- (5) Fine from a stranger, though the halo can read as self-congratulatory.
- **Verdict: loses to 🤗 on 1 and 3.**

### 8. 🥰 Smiling Face with Hearts
- (1) Very warm — hearts are an explicit show of affection — but it aims at
  *adoration* rather than a stranger's smile, so it does not beat 🤗 on
  plainly warming a stranger.
- (2) Warm at first glance, unmistakably affectionate.
- (3) Universal in the sense everyone recognises hearts, but the affection it
  signals is not universal to a stranger.
- (4) Feels picked, not default.
- (5) This is where it falls: hearts from an unknown person read as intimate
  or flirtatious, which a stranger did not ask for.
- **Verdict: loses to 🤗 on 5 (and on 1, since adoration is not a greeting).**

### 9. 💛 Yellow Heart
- (1) A heart is a symbol of warmth, not a smile aimed at someone — it is
  pleasant to receive but less likely to make a stranger actually smile than
  a smiling face with open arms.
- (2) Warm and friendly at first glance, in the friendship register.
- (3) Universal; yellow keeps it out of the romantic register.
- (4) Feels chosen — a heart is a deliberate pick over a smiley.
- (5) Mostly fine from a stranger, though any heart still carries a hint of
  affection a stranger has not earned.
- **Verdict: loses to 🤗 on 1.**

### 10. 🫶 Heart Hands
- (1) A warm gesture of sending love — close to 🤗 in intent, but it sends
  *love* where 🤗 offers a *welcome*, and a welcome is the warmer thing to
  receive from a stranger.
- (2) Warm at first glance for those who know the gesture.
- (3) The weak point: a newer gesture (Unicode 14), less reliably read by
  older strangers, and rendered inconsistently on older systems.
- (4) Feels deliberately picked.
- (5) "Sending love" from a stranger leans intimate, more so than a hug.
- **Verdict: loses to 🤗 on 3 and 5.**

### 11. 🌻 Sunflower
- (1) Sunny and cheerful, but a flower does not smile at a stranger or greet
  them — it is a pleasant object, not an offer of warmth to a person.
- (2) Warm at first glance in the sense of sunshine, not in the sense of
  kindness from a person.
- (3) Universal and inoffensive.
- (4) Feels chosen — nobody's default.
- (5) Perfectly fine from a stranger.
- **Verdict: loses to 🤗 on 1 and 2.**

### 12. 🌞 Sun with Face
- (1) Cheerful and warm like a sunny day, but the warmth is atmospheric
  rather than personal; it does not greet the stranger.
- (2) Bright and friendly at a glance, though the grinning sun face can read
  as retro or slightly uncanny.
- (3) Universal.
- (4) Feels chosen.
- (5) Fine from a stranger.
- **Verdict: loses to 🤗 on 1 and 2.**

### 13. 👋 Waving Hand
- (1) A greeting, not warmth: a wave says hello, but it does not aim to make
  a stranger smile the way a hug does.
- (2) Friendly at first glance, but the feeling is "acknowledged", not
  "warmed".
- (3) Universal.
- (4) Feels chosen over a smiley, but the choice buys a greeting, not warmth.
- (5) The safest of all from a stranger — and the least warming.
- **Verdict: loses to 🤗 on 1 and 2.**

### 14. 😺 Smiling Cat Face
- (1) Cute and gently funny, and cuteness does make people smile — but it is
  a cat being cute, not a person warming a stranger, so it aims less plainly
  at warmth than 🤗.
- (2) Warm and funny at first glance for animal lovers; less so for others.
- (3) Mostly universal, though "cat person" is a real split in how warmly it
  lands.
- (4) Feels picked — a deliberate, slightly playful choice.
- (5) Fine from a stranger.
- **Verdict: loses to 🤗 on 1 and 3.**

## Ordered comparison — why 🤗 wins

- Against every other face (😊, 😄, 😁, 😃, 🙂): 🤗 wins on criterion 1,
  because open arms are an active offer of warmth while a smile alone is
  passive — and it also wins criterion 4, because none of those faces feels
  picked.
- Against the affection entries (🥰, 💛, 🫶): 🤗 wins on criterion 1 (a
  welcome beats a declaration of love from a stranger) and on criterion 5
  (no intimacy, no flirtation).
- Against the symbols and objects (🌻, 🌞, 👋, 😺): 🤗 wins on criterion 1
  and 2, because only a face with open arms is both a smile and a greeting
  aimed at a person.
- Against 😇: 🤗 wins on criterion 1 (a greeting beats an angelic glow) and
  criterion 3 (no ironic reading of a halo).

No other candidate leads 🤗 on any earlier criterion while matching it later:
😊 is the closest, matching on 2, 3, and 5, but trailing on 1 and losing on 4.
The byte count (4 bytes, the same as every face candidate) was not a factor.

## Entry

`main.ts` writes exactly `🤗` (U+1F917, `f0 9f a4 97`) with no trailing
newline and no other bytes. Deterministic across runs.
